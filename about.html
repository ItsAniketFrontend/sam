Who I Am

About Me

I got into social media because I like having opinions that are valued. Something ignites in me when a forgettable brand becomes one people actually talk about. Not because the posts look good. Because there is now a point of view, a voice, a presence that commands attention.

Two years inside Jaipur's most demanding agencies taught me that strategy without execution is a document, and execution without strategy is noise. I have done both, for over 30 brands across food, fine jewellery, luxury real estate, healthcare, sports, fashion and D2C.

I have led teams, directed shoots, scripted brands from silence, and turned dashboards into decisions.
